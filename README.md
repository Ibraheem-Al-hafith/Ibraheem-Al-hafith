### 👤 About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer and an undergraduate student specializing in Computer Science and Statistics. My expertise spans a broad spectrum of deep learning and machine learning tasks, including Computer Vision, object detection, NLP, image classification, and audio classification. While I have a deep appreciation for software engineering principles, my current focus is on building foundational technical depth. I am actively dedicated to mastering Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), sharpening my structural time series forecasting skills, and studying Machine Learning Operations (MLOps) to understand how models live in production environments. I have a proven track record of elite execution in highly competitive data science environments, consistently ranking in the top percentiles globally for complex spatio-temporal and predictive modeling challenges.
